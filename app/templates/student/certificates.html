How certification works

Each juz moves through three steps: memorization, reciting each page to your Stage 1 Muhaffiz until the whole juz is signed off; tasmee of the entire juz to a different Stage 2 Muhaffiz within 30 days; then passed, which opens the next juz.

The 30-day clock starts when your Stage 1 Muhaffiz signs off the last page. If the whole juz is not passed inside it, tasmee for that juz starts again from page 1 — that is the 30-day rule doing its job.
